Can you work without caring about the outcome?

I do most things expecting some kind of reward or punishment. That’s just how we’re wired — classical conditioning or whatever.

I read the Bhagavad Gita recently. The part that stuck with me is Krishna telling Arjuna that to really ascend, you have to detach from the outcome and just do the thing.

I suspect most of what I call ambition is just this conditioning, not choice. I’m trying to figure out if I can actually live like that.

p. 1 · Oct 2026

Why is consumer AI so hard?

My latest realization: getting regular people to actually use AI is going to be really difficult. Only about 3% of US households pay for an AI subscription (Bank of America, 2026). Most people don’t use these tools and don’t care about the latest model.

I think products like Muse and Instinct are a good start, but I’m not convinced the “chief of staff” agent thing has broken through yet. The first consumer product that actually scales and solves real problems with AI will be very hard to build.
